## 1. Verdict

**This is ready to master.** No scene scores below 3, every rule-9 effect named in round 1 is gone, and it is now
close to the references in story and type. It sits a step below them in finish:
- light sweeps are still the cut on 7 switches;
- some 3D is still shaded CG drawers.

**The biggest weakness left** is the last three seconds of the final drop (156.5–159.4). The music is still loud
there (drums 0.64), but the frame settles to guideline motion 8–11, against 18–24 in the corridor before it. The
158.23 roll is answered only by the READ stamp at ~158.8, and no drawers shut.

**The best thing to keep** is the end of the story: "now" stays printed on the OBJ 0001 record from 145.44, then
becomes the stamp on the corridor's drawers at 150.92. The letter is stamped READ and folds into its envelope. The
envelope shrinks into the sent-mail browser at 160.06, and NEAD is stamped onto the cracked-seal card one letter per
hit and held to the end.
