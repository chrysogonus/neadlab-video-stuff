# Review: harvest-remix v21 master (`out/harvest-remix.mp4`)

Reviewer: an independent agent that built none of it (docs/REVIEW.md, step B). Inputs: `report.md`, the 21 strips,
30 fps tiles of 0.5 s around all 20 scene switches and the hook-2 drop, 60 fps frames (±4) around 15 of the unseen
hits, and 480×270 stills at the word starts I doubted. Video seconds = track seconds (offset 0). Scratch frames:
`/tmp/claude-1000/-home-chrysogonus-workspace-neadlab-video-stuff/8589e550-05f6-463c-a510-af1b96f4b4b6/scratchpad/hr-review/`.

Strip frames are cited as `scene rN.k` (row N, frame k from 0; rows from 2 on are 4 fps from the scene start).

## 1. Verdict

In ideas and insider wit this is now at the references' level, and its motion is well above both. Mosca's
inequality with "(this part is mine)", the FIPS 203 folder, the sticky-note passwords, the drawer-cake birthday, the
"DO NOT OPEN · 10 mK" fridge and the excuse roll are all the kind of detail people screenshot. The biggest weakness is
that the loudest music gets the stillest pictures. The bridge build (105–118 s, drums 0.47), with the bass return at
114.35 unanswered, and the second half of the final drop (151–160 s, drums 0.64) are the two flattest loud stretches.
On top of that, the song's payoff word "now" lands small in a corner on the 145.44 drop. The best thing to keep is
the run of literal world-object puns from 61 s to 143 s: stack, runway, monitor, cake, folder, excuse roll, fridge,
key, letter. In that run each line is one object doing exactly what the words say.

## 3. The weakest scenes, ranked

1. **library (150.92–160.06): the final drop's second half sits still.**
   - What happens: from 150.92 to ~156.9 the frame is one held glowing "now" over a dim point sphere that slowly
     dissolves (library r2–r4). The only events are flat white rings (r3.1, r5.0), which rule 9 bans. Then a small
     globe, Archie and the letter. From 159.4 there are ~0.6 s of black with one orange dot.
   - Against the music: 7 unseen hits fall here (156.4, 156.85, 157.31, 158.23, 158.54, 159.91), including the
     158.23 roll into the outro.
   - Why it matters: this is the film's peak by the music. It reads as an aftermath, and the "monumental library of
     opened records" in the storyboard never appears.
   - **Fix:** on 150.92, go through the globe's surface into rows of drawers (05's corridor, drawn as hairline line
     drawing).
     - On each downbeat (152.75, 154.57, 156.40) a whole row of drawers slams open toward the camera, and cream
       records lift out lit by their own lamps: one becomes many. BYTES READ races in the drawer labels.
     - "now" holds full-frame for one bar only, then becomes the stamp printed on each opened drawer front.
     - On 156.40 the camera finds drawer 0001 and the letter rises out of it.
     - The 158.23 roll closes the drawers in a cascade toward the lens, one per snare, ending on the letter. The
       letter then match-cuts into secure's browser card at 160.06.

## 4. Film-level fixes

1. **Make the pacing curve peak where the music does.**
   - Two stretches: the bridge build (105–118) and the final drop's second half (151–160) are the flattest loud
     music in the film. The three 4/4 stretches (drop 1 48.5–61.3, verse 2 61.3–70.3, 151–160) hold 40 of the 85
     unseen hits.
   - Apply weakest #1, #2, #4 and #6. The common rule: in loud 4/4 stretches, every kick gets an object hit inside
     the world (a lamp, a plate, a stamp, a drawer), and every phrase gets one big move.
   - The kit's kick pulse evidently doesn't reach the three.js frames (05, 22, 23). Wire it there.

2. **Set the key words as posters.**
   - Three moments: keep the opening title whole through its first bar (the push currently crops "harvest now," off
     the left edge from ~2.5 s, seal r3–r5, so a viewer arriving from the thumbnail sees the title leave). Make hook 1
     full-frame (45.49). Make "now" full-frame and single on 145.27/145.444.
   - These are the moments a viewer quotes and the thumbnail promises. The film's best poster frame, chorus 2's cream
     "now," (97.5), is currently not one of them.

3. **Replace the stock transitions and edge washes with world transitions.**
   - The same diagonal white light-sweep marks 8 of 20 scene switches (7.96, 23.66, 61.35, 75.99, 105.24, 123.48,
     134.51, 160.06).
   - Flat expanding rings recur (6.2, 33.6, 41.0, 45.3, 97.0, 152–157); the user rejected flat shock rings.
   - A warm orange left-edge leak opens wait, hook, mine, key and petabytes (strip r1 of each).
   - Replace sweeps with object matches where two scenes share an object: vault hub → dial, "later" → z, lamp → lamp,
     letter → browser. Replace rings with each object's own parts and sparks. Restrict the leak to the register flips,
     as the house style says. The last part also brings the accent area down honestly (see below).
