# Review: harvest-remix v22, draft 1 (`renders/video.mp4`)

Reviewer: an independent agent that built none of it (docs/REVIEW.md, step B). Inputs: `report.md`, the 21 strips,
30 fps tiles of 0.5 s around all 20 scene switches and both drops, 60 fps frame-difference runs at 98.9–99.9 and
across ticket and library, 10–60 fps tiles at the opening (0–7), hook 2 (96.75–98.25), harvest (51.6–53.1,
58.9–60.4), the library downbeats and the end card (162.2–164.4), 480×270 stills before and after 17 word starts,
the t = 0 frame at 168×94 and 320×180, and a per-scene accent-area pass at 2 fps. Video seconds = track seconds
(offset 0). Scratch frames: `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-chrysogonus-workspace-neadlab-video-stuff/8589e550-05f6-463c-a510-af1b96f4b4b6/scratchpad/hr-review2/`.

Strip frames are cited as `scene rN.k`: row N, frame k counted from 0. Rows 2 and later run at 4 fps from the
scene's start.

## 1. Verdict

Round 1 moved the film clearly closer to the references. Hook 1, "now", dark, qubits and drop 1 are now at their
level, and unseen hits fell from 85 to 53 with sync intact at every word start I checked. The biggest weakness is
the last 15 seconds:
- the final drop still opens on a glowing "now" held over a dissolving globe (149.4–152.7, across the 150.92 switch);
- the 158.23 roll is answered by a held letter;
- the end card has lost its artist name: N, E and A each flash for a single 60 fps frame (162.33, 162.80, 163.25)
  and no NEAD remains.

The best thing to keep is the new object-match grammar: vault hub → clock dial at 33.81, the lockup carried into
Mosca at 99.76, the wax shards flying into "now" at 145.27, and letter → envelope → browser at 160.06. That is
the transition language the rest of the film should use.

## 3. The weakest scenes, ranked

1. **secure: the end card has no artist name.**
   - What happens: the stamps on 162.32, 162.80 and 163.25 each put one big letter (N, E, A) on the card for one
     60 fps frame, and then it vanishes. D never shows. From 163.3 to 164.4 the card reads only
     "HARVEST NOW, DECRYPT LATER" in small mono, plus the cracked seal (`card-164.2.png`).
   - Why it matters: AGENTS.md requires NEAD on the end card.
   - The end-screen layout itself works. From 159.45 to the end everything sits in the left ~58 % and top ~65 %, so
     the right ~40 % and the bottom band are dark and calm for the cards and the subscribe button. The seal from
     0:00 returning cracked is a real bookend.
   - **Fix:** make the four stamps stay. N, E, A, D are inked in fire-orange rubber-stamp type, one per hit
     (162.32, 162.80, 163.25, then 163.71 into the silence), across the card's lower band at about 2× the mono
     title's size. Hold them to 164.4.
