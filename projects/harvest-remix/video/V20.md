# Harvest remix: v19 visual audit and a v20 escalation plan

## Context
You asked for an audit of the latest harvest-remix cut, its strongest and weakest scenes, and a ranked plan to raise
visual quality without a redesign. Audio and runtime (164.4 s) stay fixed. Mosca is the known weak spot. No code
changes until you approve.

**What I analysed:** the **v19 working tree** on branch `dev` (uncommitted on top of `d17adeb`), as rendered in
`projects/harvest-remix/video/renders/proof-v19-full.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, 164.4 s). No source file is newer than
that render, so it is exactly the current code. I chose v19 over the delivered master `out/harvest-remix.mp4`
(= v18) because v19 is the newest variant and is still waiting for your verdict.

**How:** I didn't re-render. I extracted frames from that proof:
- 2 fps contact sheets of the whole film;
- 5–10 fps strips at the hook, Mosca, 03→04 and 22→23;
- full-res stills at 24 points;
- per-frame luma, motion (frame difference, 320×180 gray, 10 fps) and saturation.

I then lined these up with the tracked drums and RMS (`data/audio.json`), the `HR_ENV` kick/snare/hat onsets and downbeats, and the `lyrics.json` word times.

## 2. Strongest 3

1. **05-harvest, the drawer tunnel (48.5–61.3).** Motion 23 median, 54–57 at the drop.
   - It is the only scene whose movement tracks the drums: drums 0.86–0.94, and motion peaks at 50–51 and 58–59.
   - The camera is *inside* the world, and hundreds of envelopes (the film's object) fly past at depth.
   - The "harvest" word hits on the downbeats; the flash and leak sit at 48.491.
   - Evidence: sheet cells 48.5–51.5 (tunnel, envelope storm) and 55.5–58 (hero envelope tumbling through the ring).
2. **08-mine, the exploded rotors and the hook shots (85.1–99.8).**
   - Novel image: a mechanical object comes apart into a technical drawing with deadpan callouts ("REFLECTOR · NO LETTER EVER BECOMES ITSELF").
   - The hero word "mine" is spread across the parts (still at 92.4).
   - The hook shots that follow at 96.8–99.7 ("harvest / now, now, / decrypt") are the film's best type: poster-sized, a flash and cream flip on each word, motion 28–34.
3. **17-watch, the aperture eyes (41.7–45.5).** Motion 20, peak 64.
   - A shutter opens into a single eye ("i'll see you"), then multiplies into a wall of eyes on "sooner" and closes on "later".
   - One idea, transformed on each word, at full frame. Evidence: cells 42.0–45.0.

Close runners-up: 11-petabytes (118–123), and the comedy beats in 07 (80.5–84) and 10 (107–117). Those are the most shareable frames, but they move little.

**What the best scenes share:**
- **(a)** The camera is inside or right on the object; it fills 60–100 % of the frame.
- **(b)** Something moves continuously *and* something hits on each strong onset.
- **(c)** The scene transforms at least once (tunnel → storm, drum → exploded view, eye → wall of eyes).
- **(d)** Foreground, mid and background layers move at different speeds.
- **(e)** The hero word is poster-sized and *part of* the object.

## 6. Visual guidelines draft (saved to `docs/visual-guidelines.md` after approval)

Each item can be checked on rendered frames (2 fps contact sheet plus the per-second luma/motion strip).
1. **No dead frames:** no window longer than 2 s with motion < 1.5 (still-run detector); the outro end card is the only exception.
2. **Motion follows the drums:** per-scene motion median ≥ 12 where drums ≥ 0.6, ≥ 6 in verses, 2.5–6 in deliberate breaths.
3. **Every strong hit is seen:** every onset ≥ 0.9 and every downbeat changes something visible within ±1 frame.
4. **The first frame is a poster and the first 5 s move:** t=0 legible as a thumbnail; motion ≥ 6 from 0.3 s; at least one object hit before 2.5 s.
5. **Fill the frame:** the hero object covers ≥ 35 % of the frame in every still; no small prop in a black void for more than a beat.
6. **The hero word is a poster:** ≥ 60 % of frame width on its hit, part of or on the object, nothing drawn over it.
7. **Phone-legible:** every sung line reads in a 480×270 thumbnail of the frame.
8. **One lyric layout never repeats for more than one line:** placement changes per line.
9. **Three framings per scene:** tight, mid and wide appear in each scene longer than 4 s, switched on downbeats.
10. **Depth:** at least two layers move at different speeds in every scene (parallax visible between two stills 0.5 s apart).
11. **Each scene transforms once:** the last frame of a scene differs in kind, not just position, from its first.
12. **Peaks are the brightest and fastest:** the three drops have the highest luma × motion in the film; quiet spans measurably lower.
13. **Explosions are mechanical:** parts, sparks and light come from the object; no lightning, flat rings or full-frame washes.
14. **Palette discipline:** black ground (20th-percentile luma ≤ 0.05), one accent ≤ 5 % of frame, cream 10–20 % of frames.
15. **Cuts are authored:** at least a third of scene switches are object match cuts; flashes on at most three moments.
