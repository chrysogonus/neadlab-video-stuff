# Harvest remix v16 — plan: pace, spectacle, explosive type

Requested by the user on 2026-10-18 after watching v15 ("I like v15, it needs further refinement").
Scene spans, the audio and every `lyrics.json` word start are unchanged. v15 is preserved (`out/harvest-remix-v15.mp4`,
`backups/v15-sources/`). HOUSE-STYLE rev 2 stays the base (black ground, fire-orange accent, cream, small deadpan text).

## Goals (the user's general feedback)

1. **More wow at every scene switch.** The best switch in v15 is 03 "keep": the stamp slam → pull back through the
   drawer → the vault. Every reworked scene gets that kind of switch (push-through, whip, spin, drop, match cut).
2. **More effects overall, faster pace.** Static or slow scenes are cut into 2–3 shots; no shot holds an unchanging
   layout for more than ~1.5 s; something moves on every kick.
3. **Explosive type.** Hero words are thrown in and land on their word time with a shockwave, rays, shake and flash;
   small lead words enter with varied fast moves; everything bobs on the kick. The viewer should want to dance.

## What the plan changes in the toolbox

- **`assets/kit/wow-kit.js` (`window.WOW`)** — the shared vocabulary, deterministic and seek-safe:
  `WOW.explode` (hero word), `WOW.word` (pop / drop / left / right / flip / zoom / spin entrances), `WOW.shock`,
  `WOW.rays`, `WOW.burst`, `WOW.flash`, and state helpers applied from `render()` through `WOW.draw(tl)`: `WOW.shake`,
  `WOW.punch`, `WOW.pulse` (scale + bob on kick/snare) and `WOW.transition` (paired scene-switch kinds
  `zoom · whipL · whipR · spin · drop · rise`; the outgoing frame's `out` and the next frame's `in` use the same
  kind, so the move carries through the cut). They drive CSS `translate/rotate/scale` and `filter`, so they never fight
  GSAP's `transform` on the same element.
- **Scene-switch pairs** (out of frame → into frame), each a 0.25–0.35 s move across the boundary:
  | cut | kind | idea |
  |---|---|---|
  | 01 → 02 (7.96) | zoom | the camera dives into the stamped seal; 02 rushes out of the compose window |
  | 02 → 16 (16.06) | whipL | the padlock slams shut and the frame is yanked down the wire |
  | 16 → 03 (23.656) | drop | the copy drops into the ice; 03 falls in from above |
  | 04 → 17 (41.72) | zoom | the alarm goes off and the lens pushes into the iris |
  | 13 → 14 (143.604), 22 → 23 | unchanged | 22→23 is the continuous globe handoff; 05→06 and 03→04 are match cuts that stay |
  Inside frames, shots cut with the same kinds (`WOW.transition` on a shot's group wrapper).
- **Finish layer:** more edge punches on kicks in the reworked scenes' spans.

Each scene is one frame-worker's job. "Hero" and "lead" words keep their `lyrics.json` times exactly.

## Process and verification

1. `backups/v15-sources/` holds the pre-v16 frames (done). Frame-workers get one frame each, three at a time, with
   01-seal (built first by the lead as the reference) and `wow-kit.js`.
2. Each worker looks at its frame with `./vid snap <id> <frame_id> --at …` at every shot start, every hero word
   and the last frame, and keeps `hyperframes check --no-browser-gpu` clean for its frame.
3. Whole-film `hyperframes check`, then a full 30 fps draft render `renders/proof-v16.mp4` (software WebGL), reviewed
   second by second around every switch. No commit, no push.
4. Not yet: the 60 fps delivery render and master (wait for the user's verdict on the proof).
