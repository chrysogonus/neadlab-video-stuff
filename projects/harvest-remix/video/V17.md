## The v17 motion rules (every frame)

1. **Two layers.** A *carrier* that never stops: a slow camera drift / push / crane (GSAP on a wrapper, `ease: "none"` or
   `sine.inOut`, spanning whole shots), parallax between 2–3 depth layers, objects that breathe. On top, *accents* only.
2. **Accents only on the music.** Every hand-chosen accent time passes through `WOW.on(trackT)` (snaps to the nearest kick,
   snare or downbeat within 0.12 s). Sung-word times stay exact (`lyrics.json`). No accent on anything else.
3. **Size accents by the song** (use `WOW.energy(trackT)` or the section):
   - verse (02, 16, 06, 10 first half, 13-reading): no shake, no flash, no shockwave; `WOW.pulse` amt ≤ 0.015, y ≤ 2; lead
     words `WOW.word` soft kinds (`rise | sink | glide | glideR | settle | fade`); a hero word may get one soft punch
     (s ≤ 1.04) and a thin ring or a few rays, no more.
   - pre-chorus (03 first half, 07, 21): building — pulse ≤ 0.025; one shockwave per line at most, on its hero word.
   - chorus / drop (04, 17, 18, 05, 08, 12, 14, 22): the explosive kit (`WOW.explode`, shock, rays, burst, flash) on hero
     words and downbeats; pulse ≤ 0.05.
4. **No jolts.** Shakes only on chorus/drop hero words or downbeats, amp ≤ 8, dur ≥ 0.3. No element jumps position or scale in
   one frame unless it is a deliberate hard cut. Snaps (`back.out`, `expo.out` < 0.2 s, blur > 8 px) only on chorus hero words.
5. **Shots change on the bar.** A bar is ≈ 1.83 s (131.3 BPM). Verse: about one shot per sung line (≈ 2 bars) with the camera
   travelling inside it; chorus: one shot per bar is fine. In-frame cuts sit on a downbeat (`WOW.on(t, {kinds:['down']})`)
   or a line start.
6. **Switches.** Between frames and between shots use the v17 kinds `pushIn | pullOut | glideL | glideR | glideUp | glideDown`
   with `dur: 0.46` (one beat) on each side, or a match cut / continuous camera move through an object, or a plain hard cut on
   a downbeat. Do not use the v16 kinds (`zoom | whipL | whipR | spin | drop | rise`) or blur > 8 px.
7. **No dip to black.** A frame (and an in-frame shot) is fully composed at its first instant: the move carries it in; nothing
   builds up from an empty frame at a cut. Lyrics that start later enter later, the world is already there.
8. **Fill the frame with the world, not with UI.** The lyric sits on, or as, the scene's object (HOUSE-STYLE); vary placement
   (not always small top-left with the object bottom-right). Depth: blurred foreground pieces, parallax, light. A shot is never
   more than ~30 % unused dark *and* static.
9. **Finish.** No clipped labels, no mirrored back faces (`backface-visibility: hidden`), nothing covering the lyric, the sung
   line is the most legible text on screen, no stray dots/specks. Cream frames: warm, light falloff (no grey/black vignette).

## Process and verification

1. Backup `backups/v16-sources/` (done). Kit: `wow-kit.js` gains `WOW.on`, `WOW.energy`, the soft `WOW.word` kinds and the
   v17 switch kinds (done; additive, old frames unaffected).
2. The lead rebuilds 01-seal as the reference for the v17 rules. Frame-workers then take one frame each, three at a time,
   briefed by `V17-WORKER.md`; each verifies with `./vid snap` (dense sampling around every move) and reports.
3. The lead re-measures (motion per scene, hits off the music, jolts) after every wave, runs the whole-film
   `hyperframes check --no-browser-gpu`, then renders the full 30 fps proof `renders/proof-v17.mp4` and reviews it second
   by second around every switch. No commit, no push; the 60 fps master waits for the user's verdict.
