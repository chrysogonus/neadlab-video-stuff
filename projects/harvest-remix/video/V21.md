# Harvest remix v21: lift the 10 weakest shots to the level of the best

Approved by the user on 2026-10-19 ("implement these refinements and render the video afterwards in 4k at 60 fps").
v20 sources are in `backups/v20-sources/`. Analysis: the v20 proof (`renders/proof-v20-full.mp4`), rated per shot.

## Why the best shots work (and the weak ones don't)

Measured per shot on the v20 proof: luma, p90 (90th-percentile brightness = how strong the highlights are), motion.
The 10 best have p90 74–239 (≈ 140 on average); the 10 worst 34–68. The best share five traits:
1. a tactile object from the song's world, large, lit by its own light (envelope, wax, rotors, lamps, eyes, paper);
2. real depth (camera inside the space or close on the object, parallax);
3. highlights: paper whites or white-hot accents on a black ground;
4. one becomes many, or one transforms (envelope storm, wall of eyes, exploding rotors);
5. the hero word is part of the object (lock line, stamp impression, the record).
The weak ones use abstraction (HUD rings, particles, a wireframe plexus), dark-grey materials, small objects in a void,
or blur as filler.

New rule (added to docs/visual-guidelines.md): every shot has at least one near-white or white-hot element (p90 ≥ 80),
a world object filling ≥ 50 % of the frame, and its own light source.
