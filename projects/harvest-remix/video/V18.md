## Rule changes (HOUSE-STYLE / V17 rules)

1. One accent hue, **plus at most one literal second-colour moment per film** (p(doom)'s single acid moment): the green padlock.
2. **Size accents by the measured drums** (`HR.band("drums")` / `WOW.energy`), not by the section's name: the end of
   pre-chorus 1 (drums 0.5–0.6) gets the chorus kit.
3. **Neighbouring shots never share a lyric position; each section has at least one word ≥ 60 % of the frame width.**
4. **The first cream-paper frame comes in verse 1.**
5. **The poster at t = 0 holds at most one bar**, then the story starts.
