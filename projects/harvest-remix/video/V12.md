# Harvest remix v12: plan (approved 2026-10-17, revised after the first proof)

v11 works: the ideas, the registers, the bookend. v12 adds spectacle. The weak, static moments are rebuilt as set
pieces. Scene spans, lyric times and the palette stay. v11 is preserved in `backups/v11-sources/` and
`out/harvest-remix-v11.mp4`.

## Revision after proof 1 (user, 2026-10-17)

- **Archie is not a focus.** He only replaces the lamp-headed Archivist where it already appears: 04, 12, 13, 15
  and 16. There are no new cameos, no thumbnail appearance and no new role in 09. He was taken out of 03.
- **The orange-rings chorus (08, 85.1–96.8) is the priority.** It was too static and too slow; this pass upgrades
  its look and motion and makes the data intake stunning.

## 1. Archie, the lamp replacement

`assets/kit/archie.js` (`HR.archie`) is a small, weathered cream filing-cabinet robot, inspired by WALL-E but our
own design:

- tank treads;
- a telescoping neck;
- a binocular head with fire irises, lids and brows;
- scissor arms.

He takes the Archivist's place, size and beat in each scene, with gestures as small as before. The expression sheet
is `compositions/proof/archie-sheet.html` and is not in the film.

## Revisions after proofs 2–8 (2026-10-17)

The 08 chorus centre went through four versions, all rejected: a sun, a quantum chip, a black hole and a sealed
letter. A vault with clocks was rejected too. The approved version is the **cipher bank**: six Enigma rotor drums
that step on the kicks and decelerate onto "time", "mine", "sooner", "or" and "later". It has:

- gear rims and motion blur;
- white-hot locks with sparks;
- a camera reveal on "time";
- a hex intake vortex, with data cables to BYTES HELD.

The approved proof is `renders/proof-v12i.mp4`.
