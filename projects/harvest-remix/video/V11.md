# Harvest remix v11: plan (approved 2026-10-16)

The plan comes from analysing two successful reference videos, "LGTM" and "i'm upping my p(doom)", in
`successful-videos-reference/`. Both were sampled every 2 s, and compared with v10 on cut rate, composition change,
luma and register.

**v10's gap is not pace.** v10 changes composition about as often as they do (58 % of 2 s samples, against 49 % and
68 %). The gap is in four places:

- **No literal visual pun per line.** v10 shows the archive again and again: drawer walls appear in 8 of 21 scenes.
- **One layout template.** The lyric sits top-left and the object right.
- **Two typographic voices.** There is no wit layer and no character.
- **One register.** 0 % of frames are light; the references have 9 % and 18 %.

Choruses don't escalate, and the film doesn't bookend.

## Decisions (the user agreed to the recommendations)

1. **Locks:** 06-qubits, 08-mine and 11-petabytes stay byte-identical. The film-wide counter picks up 08's own BYTES HELD
   counter on either side.
2. **Character: the Archivist.** A flat graphic filing cabinet with a desk-lamp head; its fire bulb is the eye. It
   appears in about 5 beats: 16 filing the copy, 04 waiting, 12, 13 reading, and 15 typing the reply.
3. **Fonts:** Instrument Serif Italic (the harvester's intimate voice, about 6 lines) and Caveat Brush (the archive's
   marker voice, about 8 notes). Both are OFL and bundled in `assets/fonts/`.

## Film-wide

- **One literal idea per line.** No more than 3 drawer-wall scenes.
- **Four layout families, rotated.** Neighbouring scenes never share one:
  - text on a path or surface;
  - full-bleed type;
  - screen/UI;
  - cream paper, about 15 % of runtime.
- **Annotation layer.** The archive's marker voice, wry and sparse.
- **Escalating BYTES HELD counter.** It runs across the choruses (04, 08, 12) and the drop (22).
- **Bookend.** 01 opens on the outbox ("Sent · Tue 09 Mar 2021 14:02 · padlock"). 15 closes on the same client, "READ ·
  1 TIME", with the Archivist's reply "lovely letter."
