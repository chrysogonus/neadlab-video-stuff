## Plan

### 0. Setup
- Copy the current sources to `backups/v18-sources/` (frames, kit, overlays, index). Write `projects/harvest-remix/video/V19.md`
  (this plan plus a status log) and `V19-WORKER.md` (a worker brief in the pattern of `V17-WORKER.md`).
- Rules unchanged: HOUSE-STYLE rev 3, V17 motion rules, v18's five rules. Add one lesson to HOUSE-STYLE: **explosions are
  mechanical**. Parts, sparks and light come from the object; no lightning, flat rings or full-frame colour washes.

### 1. 08-mine: the exploded view (lead builds; 85.147–96.82; the hook shots 96.82–99.756 stay)
Keep what was approved: the six canvas-sprite drums, step/seek/hold (`SEGS`, `posAt`), the intake/hex feed, BYTES HELD, the
background gears and the camera orbit. Remove the v15–v17 overload layer: the lightning canvas (strikes, charge feelers,
rotor arcs), the CSS shock rings, the `WOW.flash` washes, the screen-blend fire wash, the anamorphic streak and the glass cracks
(the ~469–680 and ~769–780 blocks and their draw calls at ~847–905).

New, all a pure function of time on the existing canvas and the `project()` camera:
- **Explode offset per rotor** along the axle (bank-local x) plus a small radial and tumble term. On each lock the bank
  pops apart (≈ 0.12 s, back.out). It holds while the word is sung, then **slams back on the next strong kick** (`WOW.on`),
  with an overshoot and a burst of sparks from the seams.
- **Inner parts revealed in the gaps**, drawn as line drawings in cream and fire: each rotor's wiring disc (24 contact dots and
  chord wires), spring contact pins, the axle, the end-cap reflector. **Deadpan callouts** on hairline leaders, IBM Plex Mono:
  `ROTOR III · 24 CONTACTS`, `REFLECTOR · NO LETTER EVER BECOMES ITSELF` (true of Enigma), `SPRING · DO NOT LOSE`,
  `AXLE · STILL TURNING`.
- **The hero word is the poster.** The separated rotors spread the locked letters across ≥ 60 % of the frame width (rule 3),
  white-hot sprite mode 1, nothing drawn over it.
- **Escalation sized by `HR.band("drums")` / `WOW.energy` (v18 rule):** "time" 88.52 partial spread. "mine" 92.22 full
  exploded view with the camera diving into the gap. "sooner" 94.111 explode with a tumble. "or" 95.438 a quick pop. "later"
  95.84 total blow-apart, the parts flying past the camera (z toward the lens) for ~1 s into the hook cut at 96.82 (the
  existing pushIn switch).
- **Mechanical light only:** seam sparks (a few dozen canvas particles with gravity), hot glow on the wiring discs that cools
  over ~0.5 s, a camera recoil ≤ 8 px (the v17 cap) on lock kicks.
- **Quality:** sprite supersampling `SS` 2 → 3 if render time allows. Motion blur as a vertical streak along the drum by speed,
  not blobs. Reframe the bank to ~80 % of the frame width and stop cropping its top and bottom. A rim and key light so the
  gunmetal reads. The lyric stays top left but moves per line (rule 3).
- Perf: canvas only, no CSS-3D. Target ≤ ~2 min to render the 15 s proof at 30 fps (v12's bank took 1m43s).
