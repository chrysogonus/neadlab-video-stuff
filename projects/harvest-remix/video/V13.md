# Harvest remix v13: plan (approved 2026-10-17, with decisions A and B as recommended)

v13 refines the delivered v12 (`out/harvest-remix.mp4`) to the level of the reference videos, following
`docs/HOUSE-STYLE.md` revision 2. The evidence is in `docs/REFERENCE-VIDEOS.md`: both references were sampled every
second, and v12 was sampled the same way for this plan (`successful-videos-reference/analysis/harvest-remix-v12/`).

**What stays:** every scene span and every sung-word time; the song's world (the archive and the victim's mail
client); the letter followed through the film; the approved set pieces (03's vault, the 08 cipher bank, 06 g4's
birthday cake, the cream 07/10 scenes); Archie, in the same small role; the music and the master. 11-petabytes stays
locked. v12 is preserved as `out/harvest-remix-v12.mp4` and `backups/v12-sources/`.

## 1. Where v12 falls short

Measured per scene on v12 (1 frame a second):

| | v12 | p(doom) | LGTM | v13 target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground (20th-percentile luma, median) | 0.078 | 0.047 | 0.063 | **≤ 0.05** |
| Pixels darker than 0.09 | 27 % | 57 % | 42 % | **≥ 40 %** |
| Accent area (median of seconds) | 6 %, with whole orange scenes | 3 % | 3 % | **≤ 5 %**, no orange grounds |
| Light (cream) frames | 12 % | 9 % | 20 % | 10–20 % (keep) |
| Composition change per second | 61 % | 86 % | 63 % | ≥ 55 % (keep) |

Scenes whose ground is the accent (median accent area): 18-hook 95 %, 23-library 72 %, 08-mine 68 %, 22-breach 38 %,
11-petabytes 29 % (locked; there the orange is lamps, which is light, so it stays).

What the frames show (every second of v12 looked at):

1. **The ground is charcoal and textured, not black.** Ink is `#111111` (luma 0.067), and almost every scene lays a
   ghost ciphertext wall, a drawer grid or a vignette glow on it. The references' black is empty.
2. **The accent is paint.** Five stretches are orange from edge to edge, and hero words are extruded orange with
   burnt steps. In the references the accent is a word, a spark or a line.
3. **The lyric often sits beside the object** (02 g2, 16, 06 g1/g3, 21, 15) instead of on it or as it.
4. **The small-text layer is thin outside 07, 09 and 10.** Those three scenes are already at reference level
   (sprint-board items, Mosca's inequality, "(sure.)"); the others have labels but few jokes.
5. **Running devices are half-built.** BYTES HELD escalates and the mail client bookends, but nothing planted early
   pays off later, and the past-tense chorus (12) doesn't visibly show the world changed since 04.
6. **Fast moves strobe.** Only the 08 drums have motion blur; the bolt slams, hook slams and camera whips don't.

**Not a goal:** more cuts. v12 already changes composition as often as LGTM; adding scenes would cost the most and
fix the least.

## 2. Film-wide changes

**F1. Black ground.**
- Ink token `#111111` → `#0A0A0A` in every frame (`--ink` and the hard-coded literals), and the finish layer's
  vignette to pure black.
- Remove ground textures: ghost ciphertext walls, drawer-grid backdrops and glow washes behind type. Texture stays on
  objects (paper fibre, metal, stamp ink).
- The drawer wall stays only where it is the subject: 05 (the corridor), 09-dark's lamps and 22/23's records.

**F2. Accent as light, never ground.**
- No orange grounds: 01 g2's flip, 03's keep panel, 18-hook, 08's chorus and hook lockup, 22, 23.
- The orange becomes what emits or marks: the seal, the sung word, lamps, the spark, the cable, the lock bars' lit
  edges.
- Hero type goes flat with a glow instead of the extruded burnt steps.
- The loud register is now black with the accent at its brightest, or a cream flash (as 08's "now," already does).

**F3. A deadpan small-text layer in every scene.** 2–4 details per scene in Plex Mono, readable when paused, all true.
They are listed per scene in section 3; every figure is checked against a source before it goes on screen.

**F4. Running devices.**
- **Plant 1, the handshake.** 01/02 show the client's footer `TLS 1.3 · X25519 · forward secrecy ✓`. In 22 the same
  line returns on the opened records with `X25519` struck through: *forward secrecy, for the forward part*.
- **Plant 2, the ticket.** SEC-0001 "Migrate to post-quantum crypto" (07, 10) pays off in 15: on the hit at
  162.32 a toast slides into the mail client, `SEC-0001 · moved to next quarter (×19)`. It is still being deferred
  after the breach. This is the film's closing joke, like LGTM's "does anybody have Greg's number?".
- **Plant 3, the passwords.** 06 g3's three re-typed fields (MAIL · BANK · CLOUD) pay off on 22's records:
  `passwords changed ×14 · irrelevant`.
- **The counter's punchline.** BYTES HELD keeps escalating (04 → 08 → 12 → 22). On "now" (145.444) its label flips
  to **BYTES READ**.
- **The chorus repeats with the world changed.** 12 is set to match 04's clock in framing and camera. Time shows:
  dust on the dial, a cobweb on Archie's neck, DAYS HELD becomes YEAR 2026, and the counter has grown to 13 digits.
- **The hook escalates.** (1) 18: clean, cream and fire on black. (2) 08 g2: heavier, with the cream flash on
  "now,". (3) 22–23: maximal, the archive lit by what it has read.

**F5. Motion blur on the snaps only.** The HyperFrames `motion-blur` component (`data-hf-motion-blur`) on one to three
slams or whips per scene: 03's bolts, 18's and 08's hook slams, 21's falling key, 22's camera move. Never on a word
that is being read; the approach blurs and the word lands sharp. The engine-wide `motionBlur` render option gets one
cost test on the proof, and is used only if it renders in reasonable time under software WebGL.

**F6. The lyric on or as the object** in the scenes marked in section 3.

**F7. A designed YouTube thumbnail.** A 1280×720 PNG from its own composition, with the title, the seal and one
insider detail. 01 stays the poster opening.

## 4. Process

1. **Prepare.** Back up v12's sources to `backups/v12-sources/`. Write SYSTEM.md **V7**: the new ink, no orange
   grounds, the small-text rules, the plants and payoffs, the motion-blur rules. Verify each small-text fact.
2. **Proof (gate).** Build the reference frames myself:
   - the opening: 01, 02;
   - the first chorus with its hook: 04, 17, 18;
   - 08's chorus on black (decision A).

   Render `renders/proof-v13.mp4` (0–48.5 s and 85.1–99.8 s). Measure it against section 1's targets, run the
   motion-blur cost test, and look at every second. **Then wait for your verdict.**
3. **Roll out.** Frame-workers, three at a time, for the remaining scenes, each with SYSTEM.md V7, the reference frames
   and `./vid snap`. I review every contact sheet.
4. **Finish.**
   - Assemble and run `hyperframes check --no-browser-gpu` (0 errors).
   - Check the source contracts (every word time unchanged; 11 locked).
   - Measure the whole film against the targets and design the thumbnail.
   - Render at 60 fps (about 75 min), master as v13, and decode-check it. v12 stays preserved.

**Rough effort:** the proof stage is several hours of build plus about 20 minutes of rendering. The rollout is a
longer working session. The final render is about 75 minutes.
